# Trestle — Submission Writeup

## The Problem

Forty-two percent of startups fail because they cannot access the resources they need. Not because the resources do not exist, but because discovering them is a full-time job buried inside an already impossible role. Founders spend thirty percent of their workweek bouncing between Crunchbase, Grants.gov, state portals, and accelerator newsletters, stitching together fragmented, outdated information. Meanwhile, billions of dollars in grants go unclaimed every year. The bottleneck is not ambition or ideas. It is search.

## The Use Case

Imagine a pre-revenue mobility founder in Detroit who needs a fifty-thousand-dollar grant to build a prototype. Today, she would spend two to three weeks manually cross-referencing eligibility matrices, checking deadlines across five government sites, and deciphering bureaucratic language. By the time she finds a match, the window is closed. Trestle collapses that two-week process into a single sentence and two seconds.

## The Solution

Trestle is an autonomous resource discovery engine for founders. You describe what you need in plain English. Trestle parses your intent, maps it against verified programs, ranks results by fit, and tells you exactly what to do next. No spreadsheets. No dead links. No guesswork.

## Target Users & Interaction

Our initial users are early-stage founders in emerging ecosystems — pre-revenue and early-revenue builders who lack the networks that Silicon Valley takes for granted. A founder arrives at Trestle and types: "I need a grant for a pre-revenue health startup in Ann Arbor." The platform returns parsed intent chips, ranked resource cards with fit scores, and actionable next steps. Each result includes an explanation of why it matches and a direct link to apply. Behind the scenes, Scout — our autonomous monitoring agent — continuously watches over five hundred sources and pushes alerts when new grants, accelerators, or pitch competitions match the founder’s profile.

## Why It Is Creative and Unique

Trestle is not another startup directory or a search engine with filters. It is an operator that understands founder intent the way a human researcher would, then acts on it at machine speed. Most tools show you a list. Trestle shows you a ranked, explained, actionable answer with a deadline attached. It closes the loop between discovery and action.

## How AI Is Used

Trestle uses a large language model for semantic intent parsing and dynamic fit scoring. The AI does not just keyword-match. It reads a founder’s query, extracts structured intent, and scores resources against that intent using custom-weighted criteria. The Scout agent runs background surveillance on hundreds of live sources, classifies new entries, and surfaces them only if they match a founder’s specific profile. This is not generative content dressed as insight. It is intelligence infrastructure that actively works while the founder sleeps.

## In Short

Trestle turns the two weeks founders waste searching into two seconds of decisive action. That is the difference between a founder who survives and one who never knew the opportunity existed.
