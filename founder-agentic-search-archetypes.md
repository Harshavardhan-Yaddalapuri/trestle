# Founder Archetypes and Search Prompts

This document defines founder examples for testing agentic internet search against fragmented and decaying founder-resource information. It uses a representative subset of `startup-funding-sources.md` rather than the full directory.

## Selected Resource Categories to Exercise

- Cloud and startup credits: AWS Activate, Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups, NVIDIA Inception.
- Accelerators and founder programs: YC, Techstars, 500 Global, Alchemist, MassChallenge, StartX, Antler, HF0.
- Non-dilutive grants: SBIR/STTR, NSF, NIH, DOE, DoD, USDA, BARDA, ARPA-E.
- Pitch competitions and prizes: Challenge.gov, TechCrunch Startup Battlefield, MIT $100K, Rice Business Plan Competition, XPRIZE, DOE American-Made Challenges.
- State/local and tax incentives: SSBCI, state economic development agencies, R&D tax credits, clean-energy credits.
- Demographic founder programs: Visible Hands, Black Ambition, StartOut, Bunker Labs, Unshackled Ventures, Gaingels, Cartier Women's Initiative, 2Gether-International.
- Physical and institutional resources: wet labs, university tech transfer, CRADAs, incubators, Manufacturing USA institutes.
- Foundation and community resources: Candid, disease foundations, Prime Coalition, Echoing Green, community foundations, CDFIs.

## Founder Archetypes

### 1. AI Infrastructure Founder

Two-person Delaware C-corp building developer tooling for AI agents, with an MVP, early GitHub traction, $5K MRR, and high compute costs.

- **Likely needs:** GPU/cloud credits, AI accelerators, technical communities, pitch competitions, customer-funded design partners.
- **Search categories:** AWS Activate, Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups, NVIDIA Inception, AI Grant, South Park Commons AI, HF0, YC, Techstars, Devpost hackathons.
- **Information needed first:** monthly compute spend, cloud provider, GPU needs, revenue, user traction, funding raised, open-source metrics, willingness to give equity, remote/in-person constraints.

### 2. Climate Hardware Founder

Graduate researcher commercializing a carbon-removal material with a working prototype, university IP questions, and expensive validation needs.

- **Likely needs:** non-dilutive grants, lab access, national lab partnerships, climate accelerators, tax incentives, pilot customers.
- **Search categories:** DOE, ARPA-E, SBIR/STTR, Activate, Breakthrough Energy Fellows, Greentown Labs, Third Derivative, Elemental Excelerator, XPRIZE, IRA credits, CRADAs.
- **Information needed first:** technology readiness level, university IP status, prototype data, climate impact metrics, lab needs, pilot partners, manufacturing location, relocation flexibility.

### 3. Biotech Diagnostics Founder

PhD-led startup developing an early diagnostic test for a chronic disease, preclinical or translational stage, needing lab space and disease-specific funding.

- **Likely needs:** NIH funding, disease foundations, biotech accelerators, wet lab space, translational research partnerships.
- **Search categories:** NIH SBIR/STTR, BARDA, JLABS, IndieBio, MassChallenge HealthTech, StartX, Cystic Fibrosis Foundation, JDRF T1D Fund, Michael J. Fox Foundation.
- **Information needed first:** disease area, regulatory pathway, IP ownership, university affiliation, validation data, principal investigator eligibility, lab/equipment needs.

### 4. Defense / Govtech Founder

Veteran founder building secure logistics or cybersecurity software for defense and commercial supply chains, with pilots but limited procurement experience.

- **Likely needs:** DoD grants, procurement pathways, set-aside support, defense accelerators, challenge funding.
- **Search categories:** AFWERX, DIU, NSIN, SOFWERX, Army xTechSearch, Navy SBIR, OTAs, CSOs, SAM.gov, APEX Accelerators, Bunker Labs, Hivers and Strivers.
- **Information needed first:** veteran or SDVOSB status, SAM.gov registration, NAICS codes, clearance posture, prior contracts, commercial traction, cybersecurity compliance.

### 5. Underrepresented Consumer Brand Founder

Black woman founder building a clean beauty or wellness brand with $20K monthly revenue, strong community traction, and retail interest.

- **Likely needs:** pitch competitions, demographic founder programs, retail partnerships, working capital, inventory financing, brand sponsorships.
- **Search categories:** Black Ambition, Visible Hands, Backstage Capital programs, Cartier Women's Initiative, Tory Burch Foundation Fellows, CDFIs, revenue-based financing, Walmart Open Call, Target Accelerator, Sephora Accelerate.
- **Information needed first:** revenue, growth, gross margins, inventory needs, founder demographics, certifications, retail pipeline, geography, equity preference.

### 6. Student Social Impact Founder

University student building an edtech nonprofit/for-profit hybrid serving low-income students, with one school pilot and unclear legal structure.

- **Likely needs:** student competitions, education foundations, fiscal sponsorship, social-impact accelerators, donation platforms.
- **Search categories:** Hult Prize, MIT Solve, university business plan competitions, Echoing Green, Strada Education, Lumina, NewSchools Venture Fund, Tides, Social Good Fund, Open Collective.
- **Information needed first:** student status, age, university affiliation, legal structure preference, beneficiaries, impact data, school partnerships, revenue/donation model.

### 7. Immigrant Fintech Founder

Immigrant founder building remittance, payroll, or credit infrastructure for migrant workers with a prototype and early compliance questions.

- **Likely needs:** fintech accelerators, immigrant founder programs, bank partnerships, financial inclusion grants, regulatory support.
- **Search categories:** Unshackled Ventures, One Way Ventures, Immigrants Rising, Mastercard Start Path, Visa Accelerator, Barclays Rise, CDFIs, Omidyar Network, financial inclusion foundations.
- **Information needed first:** immigration status if voluntarily provided, target customer geography, regulatory model, compliance readiness, pilot partners, banking partnerships, equity preference.

### 8. Rural Agtech Founder

Rural founder building sensor hardware and analytics for small farms, with farmer pilots and equipment/manufacturing needs.

- **Likely needs:** USDA grants, rural development resources, ag accelerators, equipment financing, conservation incentives, state/local programs.
- **Search categories:** USDA Rural Business Development Grants, REAP, USDA SBIR, FSA loans, Farm Credit System, FFAR, state economic development agencies, equipment leasing.
- **Information needed first:** county, farm/customer type, hardware maturity, pilot data, conservation impact, equipment needs, revenue, export ambitions.

### 9. Robotics / Advanced Manufacturing Founder

Hardware founder building warehouse robotics with a lab prototype and pilot customer interest.

- **Likely needs:** robotics accelerators, manufacturing support, R&D credits, state incentives, equipment financing, pilot programs.
- **Search categories:** HAX, MassRobotics, Bolt, Manufacturing USA institutes, NIST manufacturing programs, R&D tax credits, state hiring/investment credits, equipment financing.
- **Information needed first:** prototype maturity, bill of materials, manufacturing plan, facility needs, pilot customers, hiring plan, IP status, capital equipment needs.

### 10. Open-Source Web3 Public Goods Founder

Small open-source team building decentralized identity or privacy infrastructure, with strong GitHub activity but little revenue.

- **Likely needs:** ecosystem grants, quadratic funding, hackathons, foundation support, sponsorships.
- **Search categories:** Ethereum Foundation, Optimism RetroPGF, Arbitrum, Solana, Polygon, Near, Gitcoin Grants, Giveth, DAO treasury grants, GitHub Sponsors.
- **Information needed first:** GitHub metrics, ecosystem dependencies, legal entity status, token plans, security/audit status, public-goods rationale, prior grants.

## Cross-Archetype Intake Fields

Collect this information before asking any search agent to find resources:

- **Company basics:** name, website, entity type, incorporation state/country, operating locations.
- **Founder eligibility:** roles, voluntary demographics, student status, veteran status, immigration status, disability status, academic affiliation.
- **Stage and traction:** idea/prototype/MVP/pilot/revenue, funding raised, customers, pilots, LOIs, usage, team size.
- **Sector and technology:** industry, keywords, IP status, regulatory constraints, university or lab affiliation.
- **Resource preferences:** grants/equity/debt/credits, willingness to relocate, remote-only needs, application bandwidth, deadline urgency.
- **Use of resources:** compute, lab space, manufacturing, hiring, customer acquisition, regulatory work, pilots, equipment.
- **Disqualifiers:** prior program participation, prior grants, nonprofit/for-profit mismatch, geography restrictions, stage limits, revenue/headcount limits.

## Reusable Prompts

### Discovery Prompt

```text
You are an agentic research assistant helping a founder discover currently active startup resources.

Founder profile:
[INSERT STRUCTURED FOUNDER PROFILE]

Find relevant, currently active resources across grants, accelerators, credits, spaces, competitions, tax incentives, procurement support, demographic programs, industry programs, philanthropy, and community capital.

Instructions:
1. Search the live web and do not rely only on memory.
2. Prioritize official sources, application pages, current deadlines, and rolling admissions.
3. Return 15-25 candidates grouped by category.
4. For each resource include: name, sponsor, category, URL, status, deadline, value, equity requirement, restrictions, match rationale, source evidence, and confidence.
5. Separate strong matches from weak or speculative matches.
6. Flag stale, expired, ambiguous, or unverifiable resources.
```

### Validation Prompt

```text
You are validating startup-resource search results for freshness, eligibility, and usefulness.

Founder profile:
[INSERT STRUCTURED FOUNDER PROFILE]

Candidate resources:
[INSERT RESOURCE LIST]

For each candidate, verify the official source, current status, deadline or rolling cycle, eligibility criteria, geography, stage, entity type, demographic restrictions, and application next step.

Label each as Strong match, Possible match, Weak match, Not eligible, or Stale/unverifiable. Quote or summarize decisive eligibility evidence and produce a final top 5-10 shortlist.
```

### Gap-Finding Prompt

```text
Audit whether this founder-resource search missed important categories.

Founder profile:
[INSERT STRUCTURED FOUNDER PROFILE]

Initial search results:
[INSERT RESULTS]

Check coverage for grants, accelerators, credits, physical spaces, competitions, tax incentives, procurement, demographic programs, local/state resources, industry resources, philanthropy, and customer-funded opportunities.

For each missing category, explain why it matters, propose search queries, name likely sources or databases, and state what evidence would confirm a match.
```

### Query Expansion Prompt

```text
Generate targeted web-search queries for this founder profile:
[INSERT PROFILE]

Group queries by grants, accelerators, credits, competitions, tax incentives, physical space, procurement, demographic resources, industry resources, and local/state resources.

Include geography, stage, industry terms, synonyms, "application", "deadline", "eligibility", and the current year where useful. Avoid broad searches like "startup funding".
```

### Founder-Facing Memo Prompt

```text
Create a concise founder-facing recommendation memo from validated resources.

Include the top 10 resources ranked by fit and urgency, why each is relevant, what the founder must prepare, timing, estimated value, risks, eligibility uncertainties, suggested application sequence, and categories needing more research.
```
