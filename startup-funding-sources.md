# Startup Funding Sources

A comprehensive guide to capital sources, ranked by relevance to startups.

## Part I - Startup and Company Funding

### 1. Equity Funding

- **Pre-seed / Seed:** angel investors and angel groups such as AngelList, Golden Seeds, and Pipeline Angels; pre-seed funds such as Hustle Fund, Precursor, and Afore; accelerators such as YC, Techstars, 500, MassChallenge, and Plug and Play, typically $125K-$500K for equity; studios such as Atomic, Pioneer Square Labs, and Betaworks.
- **Venture Capital:** traditional VCs by stage, from seed through Series A and late stage; corporate VCs such as GV, Intel Capital, Salesforce Ventures, M12, and Comcast Ventures; strategic investors.
- **Alternative Equity:** equity crowdfunding through Republic, Wefunder, and StartEngine under Reg CF up to $5M; Reg A+ up to a $75M cap; family offices; sovereign wealth funds.
- **Family Offices & UHNW Individuals:** single-family offices (SFOs); multi-family offices (MFOs) such as Bessemer Trust and BBR Partners; Tiger 21 and YPO networks; AngelList, Sweater Ventures, and Republic syndicates; rolling funds; search funds for acquisition-led entrepreneurship.

### 2. Accelerators, Studios & Founder Programs

- **Top-tier general accelerators:** Y Combinator ($500K standard deal: $125K for 7% plus $375K uncapped SAFE, 3-month SF batches twice yearly); Techstars ($120K, 3-month programs); 500 Global ($150K, global); Alchemist Accelerator (enterprise, about $36K); AngelPad (NYC/SF, small batches, $120K); Entrepreneurs Roundtable Accelerator (ERA, NYC, $100K).
- **Studios and pre-accelerators:** Pioneer (remote tournament, $20K plus YC-like path); On Deck fellowships; South Park Commons ($400K founder fellowship); Antler and EF / Entrepreneur First for pre-team formation; Atomic, Pioneer Square Labs, Betaworks, and Expa venture studios; HF0 (SF residency, no equity).
- **Big Tech / corporate programs:** Google for Startups Accelerator (equity-free vertical tracks); Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub (credits and GTM, no equity); AWS Activate (up to $100K credits); NVIDIA Inception (GPU credits and technical support); Meta, Salesforce, Oracle, and IBM startup programs; Stripe Atlas for incorporation and tooling.
- **AI/ML:** AI Grant from Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross ($250K plus compute); South Park Commons AI; Conviction Embed.
- **Biotech / health:** IndieBio / SOSV ($525K, SF/NYC, deep tech bio); YC Bio track; Illumina Accelerator; JLABS / J&J (free lab space, no equity); MassChallenge HealthTech; Cedars-Sinai, Mayo Clinic, and Texas Medical Center accelerators; StartX (Stanford-affiliated, equity-free).

- **Fintech, enterprise, web3, defense, and hardware:** F-Prime, Plug and Play Fintech, Mastercard Start Path, Visa Accelerator, Barclays Rise; Alchemist, Acceleprise / Forum Ventures, Heavybit; a16z Crypto Startup School, Alliance DAO, Orange DAO, Outlier Ventures; AFWERX, NSIN, Catalyst Accelerator, In-Q-Tel, Starburst Aerospace; HAX, MassRobotics, Bolt.
- **Demographic-focused founder programs:** HBCU Founders Initiative, Founder Gym, Backstage Capital, All Raise, Female Founders Alliance, Visible Hands ($175K), Latinx Startup Alliance, Camelback Ventures, DivInc, Black Ambition, StartOut, Bunker Labs, Patriot Boot Camp, Unshackled Ventures, One Way Ventures, Immigrants Rising, Gold House, AAPI Founders Fund, Operator Collective, Native Women Lead, ONABEN, Change Labs, Indigenous Innovation Initiative, Female Founders Fund, BBG Ventures, Pipeline Angels, Golden Seeds, SheEO/Coralus, Halogen Ventures, Springbank Collective, How Women Invest, 37 Angels, Gaingels, Lesbians Who Tech, 2Gether-International, Disability:IN, Enable Ventures, Remarkable Accelerator, VetCap, Hivers and Strivers, Task Force Movement, StreetShares Foundation, Warrior Rising.
- **University-affiliated and international:** StartX; Harvard i-lab; MIT Delta v; Cornell eLab; NYU Endless Frontier Labs; Creative Destruction Lab; Berkeley SkyDeck; EF, Antler, Seedcamp, Startupbootcamp, Founders Factory, Station F, Sequoia Surge, Z Fellows, Iterative, 8200 EISP, The Junction.
- **Non-equity communities:** South Park Commons, On Deck, HF0, Founders Inc, AGI House, Indie Hackers, and Z Fellows ($10K, 1 week, no equity).

### 3. Non-Dilutive Funding (Grants & Awards)

- **US federal programs:** SBIR/STTR across NIH, NSF, DOE, DoD, NASA, USDA, DOT, ED, EPA, DHS, and DOC, about $4B/year with Phase I ($50K-$300K), Phase II ($750K-$2M), and Phase III commercialization; NSF Convergence Accelerator, Partnerships for Innovation, and I-Corps; NIH R01, R21, R43/R44 and institute grants; DOE ARPA-E, loan guarantees, and Office of Technology Transitions; DoD DIU, AFWERX, SOFWERX, Army xTechSearch, and Navy SBIR; NASA iTech and Tipping Point; USDA Rural Business Development Grants and REAP; EPA Green Chemistry; NIST manufacturing programs; DARPA, IARPA, and BARDA.
- **State and local:** state economic development agencies; state SBIR matching; city innovation funds such as NYCEDC, LA, and Boston; Opportunity Zones; state R&D, hiring, and investment tax credits; SSBCI programs in every state.
- **International grants:** EU Horizon Europe, EIC Accelerator up to EUR17.5M, and Eureka; UK Innovate UK and British Business Bank; Canada IRAP, SR&ED, and Strategic Innovation Fund; Israel Innovation Authority; Germany EXIST and ZIM; Singapore Enterprise Singapore and Startup SG; France Bpifrance and La French Tech; Australia R&D Tax Incentive and Accelerating Commercialisation; Japan J-Startup and NEDO.

### 5. Debt & Revenue-Based Financing

- Venture debt from Silicon Valley Bank successors, Hercules, and TriplePoint; revenue-based financing from Capchase, Pipe, Lighter Capital, and Clearco; SaaS financing from Founderpath and Arc; SBA 7(a), 504, and microloans; CDFI loans; equipment financing; invoice factoring; Brex, Mercury, and Arc lines of credit.

- **Asset-based financing:** equipment leasing; inventory financing; accounts receivable factoring; purchase order financing; warehouse receipts; IP-backed loans; real estate financing.

### 6. Customer & Revenue-Based Capital

- Pre-orders and deposits, such as the Tesla model with $1K-$10K deposits; LOIs that become financing through Pipe or Capchase; annual prepay discounts; customer-funded development; design partnerships where early enterprise customers pay $50K-$500K; milestone payments from large government or enterprise contracts.

- **Government direct investment:** In-Q-Tel, AFVentures, NavalX, Army Applications Lab, NSIN, state sovereign-wealth-style funds, CHIPS Act direct funding, and DOE Loan Programs Office ($400B+ available).

### 24. Time-Specific Emergency Funding

## Notes on Using This List

- **Stage and structure determine eligibility.** A pre-revenue solo founder qualifies for different sources than a Series A team of 40; a YC-backed Delaware C-corp may not apply directly for most foundation grants but can win prize challenges or receive PRIs.
- **Stack non-dilutive sources before equity.** SBIR, R&D tax credits, cloud credits, and pitch competition wins can add up to $1M+ before giving up shares.
- **Geographic and local sources are a major blind spot.** Programs such as Detroit Economic Development Corp grants, Wayne County minority business funds, and Michigan Strategic Fund programs change often and require local intelligence.
- **Demographic eligibility unlocks parallel pools.** Set-asides such as 8(a), WOSB, HUBZone, and SDVOSB, plus demographic-focused capital from Backstage, Visible Hands, Unshackled, and Cartier, are often underused.
- **Customer-funded development is underrated.** Pre-orders, LOIs, design partnerships, and milestone payments fund growth without dilution or debt service while validating product-market fit.
